I am a Ph.D. candidate in Atmospheric Chemistry at the University of Montana.

My research focuses on atmospheric emissions and chemistry associated with biomass burning and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). I am particularly interested in prescribed fire emissions, air quality, atmospheric chemistry, chemical instrumentation, and field measurements.

## Research Interests

- Prescribed fire and wildfire emissions
- Volatile organic compounds (VOCs)
- PTR-ToF-MS measurements
- Air quality
- Atmospheric oxidation chemistry
- Aerosol and trace-gas measurements
- Field instrumentation and method development

## Current Research

My current research focuses on characterizing gaseous and particulate emissions from prescribed fires in the western United States. I use mobile field measurements that combine PTR-ToF-MS with trace-gas, aerosol, and meteorological observations to investigate emission characteristics and their potential impacts on atmospheric chemistry and air quality.

I am also interested in the development and application of advanced mass spectrometry methods for atmospheric VOC measurements.

## Research Experience

My work includes:

- Field measurements of prescribed-fire emissions
- VOC characterization using PTR-ToF-MS
- Measurements of CO, CO2, CH4, NOx, O3, particulate matter, and black carbon
- Instrument calibration, troubleshooting, and method development
- Atmospheric data analysis using R

## Selected Projects

### SMART-FIRES

Characterization of gaseous and particulate emissions from prescribed fires using a mobile laboratory equipped with PTR-ToF-MS and complementary trace-gas and aerosol instrumentation.

### Volatile Chemical Products

Investigation of volatile organic compounds associated with volatile chemical products and their contribution to atmospheric reactivity.
